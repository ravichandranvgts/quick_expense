# Quick Expense – Approver Guide

**For:** branch / department heads, Accounts Managers and Finance Managers who approve bills.

## Approve or reject

1. Open the notification (bell or email), or the Journal Entry list filtered on *Approval Status = Pending Approval*.
2. Check the bill attachment against: supplier name, invoice number and date, amounts and category.
3. Read the top of the form: warnings and *Accounts Manager approval needed* reasons.
4. Click **Approve** (comment optional) or **Reject** (reason required). On a phone the buttons are at the top of the form; in the Journal Entry list tick several bills and use **Actions › Approve Quick Expense**.

When the last level approves, the entry posts automatically.

## What you may change

* Approvers: **Remarks** and **Cost Center** only.
* Accounts Managers: also **ITC Eligibility** (with a reason), reverse charge and place of supply.
* Changing an amount sends the entry back to Level 1 for everyone to see again.

## You cannot approve when

* you created the entry yourself;
* the level needs a different role or a named person;
* you have no permission for that company or GSTIN.

The form tells you which of these applies.

## Accounts Manager only

* **Retry Posting** when an entry shows *Approved – Posting Failed* (fix the cause shown in red first).
* **Cancel with Reason** on a posted entry; a reason is required when the bill is already reconciled with GSTR-2B.
* GSTR-2B: open India Compliance's **Purchase Reconciliation Tool**, pick the Company GSTIN and period, click **Reconcile**. Quick Expense bills show as *Journal Entry* rows; use *Accept*, *Pending*, *Ignore*, *Link* or *Unlink* as usual. The bill's Reconciliation Status updates by itself.
* Entries pending more than two days are escalated to the next level and to Accounts Managers.

## Onboard a supplier from a bill

Open the Quick Expense Journal Entry and click **Create Supplier** (shown only when the supplier is not yet in the Supplier master). Check the details and addresses shown (taken from the GST portal), then confirm. A supplier with the same GSTIN is reused instead of being created twice. You need the *Purchase Master Manager* or *Purchase Manager* role.
