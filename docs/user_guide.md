# Quick Expense – User Guide

**For:** staff who enter expense bills.

## Enter a bill (2 minutes)

1. **Quick Expense › New Quick Expense Bill** (a Journal Entry with Voucher Type *Quick Expense*).
2. **Company GSTIN** – the GSTIN printed on the bill as the buyer. Tick **Bill is addressed to the selected Company GSTIN** only if it really is.
3. **GST Treatment** – *Registered Regular* for most bills; *Unregistered* for shops without a GSTIN; *Overseas* for foreign online services.
4. **Supplier GSTIN** – type it; name, address and state fill in from the GST portal and are locked. If the portal is not reachable, the name stays open for you to type (an Accounts Manager will then approve the bill).
5. **Supplier Invoice No and Date** – exactly as printed. Tick **Is Reverse Charge** for GTA freight, advocates and similar.
6. **Rows** – one per expense: Expense Account, **Taxable Amount** (before GST), **GST Rate**, HSN / SAC, and **Eligibility for ITC** if it is not eligible (food, cab hire … = 17(5)). Keep **one Cash or Bank row** for the payment (your default is added for you); its amount is filled on save. For bank, fill Reference No and Date.
7. Attach the bill, **Save**, check the totals at the top, then click **Submit for Approval**.

## After submission

* **Pending Approval (1/2)** – waiting for the approver shown at the top.
* **Rejected** – read the reason in *Approval History*, correct, save and submit again.
* **Posted** – done; the GSTR-2B status shows at the top.
